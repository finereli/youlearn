# Roadmap

## Next

- **Parent console.** Manage which shows, seasons and videos a child can see:
  paste a playlist, channel or video link, pick episodes, reorder. The
  library moves from the bundle to the server (D1), so every device gets it.
- **Parent and child accounts.** A parent signs in (finereli login) and owns
  one or more child profiles. The child's device opens straight into their
  profile and can't reach the console without the parent.

## Later

- **Watch time and schedule.** Daily limits, allowed hours, an "that's it for
  today" card when time is up, set per child from the console.
- **Progress across devices.** Resume positions sync per child instead of
  living in each device's localStorage.
- **Offline episodes** for flights and car trips, if there's a clean way.
- **Public release** once accounts and the console exist.
